FAQ

Questions businesses ask

What can Insight Pros help our business with?

We help with websites, digital marketing, content, customer communication and business automation. We also build custom applications and connect business tools. The first conversation helps identify which work fits your needs.

Do you work with small and larger businesses?

Yes. We discuss the size of your team, the people involved and the systems you use before recommending a project. Work can focus on one business need or several connected processes.

Can we start with just one service?

Yes. You can start with a website, campaign, communication workflow or another defined priority. We can discuss additional work as your needs develop.

Can you work with our current software?

We review the tools you use and the access they support before proposing a connection. If an integration has limits, we explain them as part of the plan.

How much does a project cost?

Cost depends on the scope, systems and support involved. Your proposal explains the project charges and any recurring software, usage or service fees. Advertising spend is identified separately when relevant.

How long does the work take?

The schedule depends on what is being built, the content needed and the systems involved. We agree on milestones and responsibilities before work begins.

What happens if an automated conversation needs a person?

We agree on handoff rules during setup. Depending on the system, that may mean taking a message, creating a task or directing the customer to your team.

What do SEO, AEO and GEO mean?

SEO improves how your website is found and understood in search. AEO helps content answer customer questions clearly. GEO focuses on how your business information can be discovered and referenced in generated answers. None guarantees a ranking or recommendation.
