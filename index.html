01 / The missed-call leak

$26,000

Estimated revenue at risk per year

A missed call is often a customer calling your competitor.

When a call goes unanswered, many callers don't leave a voicemail. They try the next business on the list. For many local service businesses, we estimate a missed call is worth about $100 in lost revenue. At that rate, five missed calls a week adds up to more than $2,000 a month.

An estimate at $100 per missed call × 52 weeks. Your numbers will vary.

An AI voice agent can help you answer more of those calls, without hiring another receptionist.

07 / Common questions

The questions owners ask us most.

What does Insight Pros build?

We build websites, AI agents (phone, text and email) and custom tools that connect your website, phone, calendar, CRM and forms. The goal is simple: handle more inquiries with less manual work.

What is Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)?

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) is the practice of structuring your business information and website content so that answer engines (Google AI Overviews, featured snippets, voice assistants like Siri and Alexa, and AI chat tools) can understand it and quote it directly as the answer to a customer's question.

What's the difference between SEO, AEO and GEO?

SEO helps your website rank in traditional search results on Google and Bing. AEO helps your content get quoted as the direct answer in featured snippets, AI Overviews and voice search. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) helps AI assistants like ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Copilot recommend your business by name. We combine all three.

What is an AI voice agent and how does it work?

An AI voice agent can answer your business phone at any hour, including nights and weekends. It speaks with callers naturally, answers common questions, books appointments directly into your calendar and CRM, takes messages, and can make outbound calls to confirm appointments and follow up on quotes.

How much does a missed call cost a small business?

It varies by business. For many local service businesses we use an estimate of about $100 per missed call, because many callers who reach voicemail try another business. At that estimate, five missed calls a week adds up to more than $2,000 a month.

What's the difference between an AI chatbot, an AI agent and missed-call text back?

An AI chatbot answers questions in a chat window on your website. An AI agent goes further: it takes actions such as booking appointments, updating your CRM and making phone calls. Missed-call text back automatically texts anyone whose call you couldn't answer, so the conversation continues instead of going to a competitor. For a dental office, together they mean no patient inquiry goes unanswered.

How can you help a home services business like a contractor, roofer or plumber?

We help home services businesses with a website that brings in calls, Google Maps and AI search visibility, an AI voice agent to answer more calls, automatically follow up on quotes, request and respond to Google reviews, and keep the schedule full, so your team spends its time on jobs instead of the phone.

How do Google reviews and reputation management help my business?

Recent, positive Google reviews are an important signal for local search, and they help new customers trust you. We can send review requests after each job and help you respond to reviews.
